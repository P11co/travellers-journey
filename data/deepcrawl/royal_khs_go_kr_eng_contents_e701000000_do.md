Free Admission Guidelines for Hanbok Wearers
한복무료관람가이드라인 안내 테이블 - 구분, 세부내용으로 구성 Category | Details  
---|---  
General |  1. Both traditional Hanbok and modernized Hanbok are eligible for free admission.  
2. The basic requirement is to wear both an upper garment (jeogori) and a lower garment (skirt or pants). Simply wearing an overcoat (durumagi) is not recognized as Hanbok form; both upper and lower garments must be worn.  
※ Hanbok must be worn according to the following guidelines and in a proper manner. (e.g. Keep your upper garment (jeogori) on at all times since wearing the revealing Hanbok is against the public decorum.)  
details | upper garment (Jeogori) |  3. All upper garment (jeogori) ribbon knots(goreum) and buttons should be fastened or tied at all times.   
(T-shirt type Hanbok is not allowed)  
All types of lower garments (skirt or pants) should cover the legs. ![저고리 이미지](https://royal.khs.go.kr/imgs/images/2024/07/19/20240719093500317_ITCFOTJ4.png)  
lower garment pants (baji) |  4. The pants are as follows. ![](https://royal.khs.go.kr/imgs/images/2024/07/19/20240719094041578_FZJ701FT.png)  
lower garment skirt (chima) |  5. All types of Hankbok skirts are available but must be worn with a separate upper garment(jeogori) at all times.  ![치마이미지](https://royal.khs.go.kr/imgs/images/2024/07/19/20240719093500351_9L8IVJI7.png)![치마이미지](https://royal.khs.go.kr/imgs/images/2024/07/19/20240719093500356_W13MC203.png)  
  * * Partly-worn Hanbok (e.g. upper garment (jeogori) with jeans or lower garment (skirt or pants) with a t-shirt cannot be entitled to free admission.
  * * Wearing a partially worn Hanbok (e.g., pairing the upper garment (jeogori) with jeans or the lower garment (skirt or pants with a t-shirt) does not qualify for free admission.
  * * We reserve the right to refuse entry to any individuals dressed inappropriately or who misbehave.
  * * We reserve the right to refuse entry to individuals who are dressed inappropriately or who misbehave.
